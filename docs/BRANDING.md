# SPANCHOR Brand

## Official Logo

The official SPANCHOR logo represents the core identity of the project: stable source-anchored retrieval evaluation for RAG systems.

## Symbol

The symbol is a stylized S-shaped ribbon with anchor-like rounded endpoints, representing:

- **S-curve**: The flow of retrieval evaluation from source documents through to results
- **Ribbon**: Continuity and stability of source anchors across retrieval changes
- **Rounded endpoints**: Anchor points — stable reference positions in canonical source text

The design conveys technical sophistication, reliability, and modern architecture.

## Wordmark

The wordmark "spanchor" uses a two-tone color scheme:

- **span**: Deep navy blue (#001a4d)
- **chor**: Bright blue (#0066ff)

This reflects the visual separation between "span" (character intervals) and "anchor" (anchored evaluation), while maintaining cohesive brand identity.

## Color Direction

The logo uses a clean blue/cyan gradient palette:

- **Primary**: Cyan to deep blue gradient (representing the flow and stability of anchors)
- **Accent**: Deep navy for emphasis and contrast
- **Background**: Clean white or transparent

The color scheme conveys:
- Trust (blue)
- Technology (cyan)
- Professional reliability (deep navy)

## Usage

### Preferred Sizes

- **Large displays** (documentation, websites): 600px or larger
- **Medium** (README badges): 200–400px
- **Small** (favicons, social): 64–128px
- **Minimum practical**: 64px (readability threshold)

### Light Background Usage

Use the primary logo on white or light gray backgrounds. The logo renders with good contrast and maintains clarity at all supported sizes.

### Dark Background Usage

For dark backgrounds or dark-mode interfaces, use a white or light variant if available, or ensure sufficient contrast is maintained.

### Guidelines

- **Do not stretch**: Maintain aspect ratio
- **Do not distort**: Keep proportions consistent
- **Do not recolor**: Use official colors only
- **Do not add effects**: No arbitrary shadows, glows, or filters
- **Do not invert**: Never invert the logo for dark backgrounds
- **Spacing**: Maintain clear space around the logo (minimum 10% of logo height)

## Brand Voice

SPANCHOR communicates:

- **Technically precise**: Exact terminology, no marketing fluff
- **Developer-focused**: Practical, implementation-oriented
- **Reliable**: Deterministic, local-first, reproducible
- **Open**: Community-driven, transparent processes
